The honest verdict

Can you do it without 402?

Partly — and then you've rebuilt x402.
You can skip the 402 status code — but not the handshake. And the moment you rebuild the handshake, a private one is strictly worse than the open standard PipRail already speaks.
Dynamic pricing needs a handshake
A static doc serves one fixed price. Per-request pricing forces a fresh signed spec per call — a challenge by another name.
Interop you can't build alone
A private dialect is invisible to x402 indexes and breaks every off-the-shelf client. The network is the asset — you adopt it, you don't rebuild it.
Where PipRail actually innovates
Not new crypto — a settlement scheme: pay first, prove with a tx ref, verify locally on your own RPC (no facilitator, no fee) — on the standard envelope.
A bare transfer is cash left on a restaurant table with no check — you're guessing the total, and nothing proves this cash settled this meal. x402 is the itemized check the kitchen hands you (exact amount, who to pay, a one-time table number) verified against the kitchen's own ticket — never your word. Reconstruct it yourself and you'll print the same check, just one no other restaurant accepts.
